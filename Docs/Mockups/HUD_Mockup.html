Expedition: Deepshaft — example HUD

Interactive mockup for the v3.4 design (Gold / Iron / Silver, shards, Smith, automated Expeditions, no tickets, no stockpile cap). Tap resources for tooltips, switch tabs, press Collect. The device is the 1080 × 2400 canvas at ⅓ scale, so every size here × 3 = canvas units.

HUD anatomy

  1. Resource bar (always on top). Gold and Iron always; Silver from Tier 3, Mithril from Tier 5. Locked resources are hidden, not greyed, to keep the bar short.
  2. Shards as a pair: Basic (cyan, letter B) and Advanced (violet, letter A). Shape + letter, not only color.
  3. ▾ on the shards chip opens the full inventory: rare Expedition materials, boss materials and Essence. They get no slot of their own, so the bar fits 360 px even at Tier 5.
  4. Tier line under the bar: current mine tier and what is next (boss to beat).
  5. Tabs: HQ · Mine · Expedition · Minions · Smith · Boss. Badges only for "something to do": mining waiting, a finished first clear, an affordable upgrade.
  6. One primary button per card (gold), placed bottom-right for the right thumb.

v3.4 rules shown here

  1. Mine has no cap: it shows "waiting to collect", which just keeps growing (watch it tick). No "FULL" state.
  2. Only one Expedition at a time. When automated, the Adventure team is Busy, and Boss offers Stop Expedition & Fight.
  3. Boss attempts are free: no ticket counter.
  4. Smith shows the next breakthrough and exactly what is missing ("need 6 more Spore Silk").
  5. Summon picks pool (Miner / Adventure) and shard (Basic / Advanced), with rates on screen.

Sizes (canvas units)

  1. Touch targets ≥ 130 (44 px here)
  2. Resource numbers 44 bold · body 40 · secondary 32
  3. Primary buttons 132 tall here; go to 160 in Unity
